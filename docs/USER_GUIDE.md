# Welcome to Console: Your User Guide

Welcome aboard! We built this platform to make scheduling content across your social media channels as painless as possible. No more jumping between different apps or losing track of your media files. 

Here is everything you need to know to get your first automated post out the door.

---

## 1. Creating Your Account
When you first visit the platform, you'll need to create an account. Simply head to the **Sign Up** page, enter your email and a secure password, and you're in. 

Because we process your videos and images on our own servers, your account comes with its own private media library. Everything you upload is totally isolated and secure.

## 2. Connecting Your Social Accounts
Before you can schedule a post, you need to tell us where to publish it!

1. Go to the **Accounts** page from the sidebar menu.
2. Click the **"Connect Facebook"** button.
3. A popup window will appear asking you to log into Facebook. 
4. Facebook will ask you which Pages and Instagram accounts you want to grant us access to. **Make sure you select them all!**
5. Once approved, the window will close, and you'll see your Facebook Pages and linked Instagram Business accounts appear as active cards on your screen.

*(Note: If you want to post to Instagram, you must ensure your Instagram account is set to "Professional" or "Business" mode on your phone, and that it is linked to your Facebook Page in the Facebook settings).*

## 3. Uploading Your Media
We feature a central media library where you can store all your assets before building posts.

1. Head over to the **Media** tab.
2. Click **Upload** and select the images or videos you want to use.
3. Once they finish uploading, they are safely stored in your cloud library. You can hover over any item to preview it or delete it if you uploaded the wrong file.

## 4. Scheduling Your First Post
This is where the magic happens!

1. Go to the **Composer** tab.
2. **Select your Media:** Click the "Select Media" button to browse your private cloud library and pick the image or video you want to post.
3. **Write your Caption:** Type out the caption you want your audience to see.
4. **Choose your Platforms:** Check the boxes next to the Facebook Pages or Instagram accounts you want this post to be published to.
5. **Pick a Time:** Use the date and time picker to schedule exactly when this should go live. (Make sure your timezone is correct in the Settings tab!).
6. Click **Schedule Post**.

## 5. Tracking Your Posts
Want to see what's coming up?

- **The Dashboard** gives you a bird's-eye view of your publishing velocity, showing you what is scheduled for today and what has already successfully gone out.
- **The Calendar** gives you a full monthly view so you can visually balance your content schedule.
- **The Logs** tab is your best friend if something goes wrong. If Facebook rejects your post because your video was too long or your image format was unsupported, the exact error will show up in the Logs so you know exactly how to fix it!

Happy publishing!
